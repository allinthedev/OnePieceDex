The player guide is written to be posted on the server as it is.

## What they cover

The Christmas event — the pass and its snowflakes, the advent calendar, the Secret Santa, the crew
sleigh — and the features that were built alongside it: the boosts the merchant sells, the Discord
role a crew carries, several frames on the same treasure, repeatable collector claims, and the
card credits.

## Two things they will not tell you

- **The pass file.** `eventexample/christmas_2026.json` builds the whole pass in one command;
  `eventexample/README.md` is its documentation.
- **Whether a Secret Santa parcel was filled by the bot.** The panel knows, and that is where it
  stops. The staff guides explain why.
